Most Medway schools have the same holiday dates, but some academy, voluntary-aided or foundation schools may differ. If in doubt, contact the individual school.
The term dates below give a total of 195 days. Maintained schools must provide 190 teaching days, with the remaining 5 days used for staff development.
Each school is free to decide when these staff development days take place. Academies have the authority to set their own term dates.
