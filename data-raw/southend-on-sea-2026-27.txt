School term and holiday dates
These dates are for community schools in the city of Southend-on-Sea, namely:
Chalkwell Hall Infant
Earls Hall Primary
Fairways Primary
Leigh North Street
West Leigh Infants
Most schools in the city follow the dates however, term dates for foundation and voluntary-aided schools and academies may be different. Check with your child's school for their term dates.
