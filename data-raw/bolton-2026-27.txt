Dates for 2026/27 holiday pattern 

Term dates may vary slightly for each school, please check with individual schools for their holiday dates. (Term dates are published on school websites)

Autumn Term 	Total 74 days in term 

Term begins: 	Tuesday 1st September 2026
Half Term: 		Monday 26th October – Friday 30th October 2026
Term ends: 		Friday 18th December 2026

Spring Term 	Total 54 days in term 

Term begins: 	Monday 4th January 2027
Half Term: 		Monday 15th February – Friday 19th February 2027
Term ends: 		Thursday 25th March 2027

Summer Term 	Total 69 days in term 

Term begins: 	Monday 12th April 2027
Bank Holiday: 	Monday 3rd May 2027
Half Term: 		Monday 31st May (Bank holiday) – Friday 4th June 2027

Term ends: 		Friday 23rd July 2027

Additional Information: 

	•	Children will be in school for 190 days. 
	•	Staff will be in school for 195 days.
	•	5 INSET days to be allocated by the school during non-term time. 
	•	The above pattern equates to 197 days and Governing Boards have the authority to determine 7 occasional days to meet the local needs of the school.
	•	There is a 6-week summer break leading into this year with a 6-week break at the end.
	•	Start of next academic year is Monday 6th September 2027.
	•	Bank holidays for this year are 

Christmas Day
Friday 25th December 2026
Boxing Day 
Saturday 26th December 2026
New Year’s Day 
Friday 1st January 2027
Good Friday
Friday 26th March 2027
Easter Monday 
Monday 29th March 2027
May Day Bank Holiday 
Monday 3rd May 2027
Spring Bank Holiday
Monday 31st May 2027
August Bank holiday 
Monday 30th August 2027

	•	Local Elections are due to take place on Thursday 6th May 2027. (If the school is used as a polling station, and the school is required to close to facilitate the electoral duties of the polling station, then one of the occasional days should be allocated to this closure.)

School year regulations
Schools must open for 380 half-day sessions (190 days) in each school year, beginning with the first term to start after July.
We are responsible for setting holiday and term dates for community and voluntary controlled schools. The term dates for foundation and voluntary aided schools are decided by their governing bodies.
