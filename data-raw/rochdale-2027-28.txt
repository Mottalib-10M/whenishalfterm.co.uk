2027–2028 school holidays
This is our recommended school holiday pattern for September 2027–August 2028.
Autumn term 2027–2028
There are 14 weeks in this school term.
Autumn term begins: Monday, 6 September 2027
Autumn half-term holiday: Monday, 25 October 2027–Friday, 29 October 2027 inclusive
Autumn term ends: Friday, 17 December 2027
Christmas holiday: Monday, 20 December 2027–Friday, 31 December 2028 inclusive
Spring term 2027–2028
There are 13 weeks in this school term.
Spring term begins: Tuesday, 4 January 2028
Spring half-term holiday: Monday, 21 February 2028–Friday, 25 February 2028 inclusive
Spring term ends: Friday, 7 April 2028
Easter holiday: Monday, 10 April 2028–Friday, 21 April 2028 inclusive
Summer term 2027–2028
There are 11 weeks in this school term.
Summer term begins: Monday, 24 April 2028
Summer half-term holiday: Monday, 29 May 2027–Friday, 2 June 2028 inclusive
Summer term ends: Friday, 21 July 2028
Summer holiday: Monday, 24 July 2028–Thursday, 31 August 2028 inclusive

About school terms, holiday dates and closures
We give schools in the borough a recommended school holiday pattern for the academic year.
Check exact dates with your school
Every year schools are required to choose 3 days during term time to set aside for staff training which students don't attend.
We also allow schools to change up to 2 days of our recommended holiday pattern, for example for polling days or religious festivals. You'll need to refer to your specific school's website for the exact term dates.
