Term dates
We set term dates for community and voluntary controlled schools. 5 training days are chosen at the discretion of individual schools every academic year. Please check your school's website as needed.
Alternative dates
Academies, free, foundation and voluntary aided schools may choose their own term dates. Please check school websites.
