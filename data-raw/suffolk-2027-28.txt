The school year for community and voluntary controlled schools includes 195 days in total. 
Schools set five Professional Development (PD) days for staff training, which means there are 
190 teaching days in the school year. 
 
Suffolk County Council (SCC) recommends the following PD days: Wednesday 1, Tuesday 2 
and Friday 3 September 2027, Tuesday 4 January 2028 and Tuesday 18 April 2028.  
 
Most schools in Suffolk follow these dates but you should contact your school or check their 
website to confirm. 
 
Please note: voluntary aided, free schools and academies can set their own term dates so 
these may differ from those recommended by SCC. please check with your child's school in 
case these differ from those set by us. All term dates for community and voluntary controlled 
schools are available on SCC’s website at www.suffolk.gov.uk. Where a school has chosen 
different term dates, this information will be published by the school.
