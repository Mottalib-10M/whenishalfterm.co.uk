Devon County Council consults on, determines and publishes term dates that are recommended to all state-funded schools and academies in the county. They can then be used as the basis for the term dates for schools themselves.
On this page, you will see the determined term dates for this academic year and the coming years. They allow for 197 days on which children could attend school. You do not have to follow them exactly although the closer schools follow them, the easier it is for parents with children at different schools to co-ordinate attendance.
All maintained schools are required by The Education (School Day and School Year) (England) Regulations 1999 to open for 195 days, of which pupils should attend for 190 days during each academic year.
Schools should set two days of occasional holiday and five staff training days from the 197 recommended by the Council. Academies and free schools are not bound by these regulations but are encouraged to provide the same number of education days in each year.
It is recommended that schools set their occasional days to avoid split week holidays, wherever they fall, however, this is the responsibility of school governors, not the Council.
Schools are asked to indicate their non-pupil dates and opening times to the County Council after the core dates have been confirmed and published on this website. For the dates and times set for all Devon state-funded schools, please go to the School information pages .
You can access a spreadsheet to mark your term dates if you wish. Each tab has a different academic year.
