1. Foundation Trust, Academies and Voluntary Aided (faith) schools are not obliged to

Term and Holiday dates for 2027 -2028 
 
 
 
   
 
2. Maintained schools are usually open for the teaching of pupils for 190 days in a 
school year.  This calendar shows 19 2 calendar days.  The additional 2 days will be 
used by schools for non-teaching days; schools are closed to pupils on these days.  
3. Schools must identify 3 further non-teaching days or the equivalent  outside of these 
specified term dates. 
4. The dates of the non-teaching days are not necessarily the same across all schools.  
Individual schools will notify parents of the dates when school is closed to pupils for 
non-teaching days. 
5. Schools must take account of closures to accommodate local or Parliamentary 
elections when setting non-teaching days. 
6. Schools are likely to re-open on  Monday 4th September 2028.
