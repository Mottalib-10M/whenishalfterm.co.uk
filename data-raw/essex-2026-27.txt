School term dates and holidays for academic year 2026 to 2027 for community and voluntary controlled schools in Essex
These dates are for community and voluntary controlled schools in Essex.
Term dates for foundation and voluntary-aided schools and academies may be different. Check with your child's school for their term dates.

There are 195 school days in a year.
Schools also have 5 days out of the academic year for staff training. Contact your child’s school to find out when they will be.
View the full 2026 to 2027 term date calendar (PDF, 211.25 KB) .
You can find bank holidays dates on GOV.UK.
You must get permission from the headteacher if you want to take your child out of school during term time .
