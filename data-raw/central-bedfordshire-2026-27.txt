2026 to 2027 school year
Autumn term 2026
term starts on Thursday, 3 September 2026
half-term is Monday, 26 October to Friday, 30 October 2026
term starts again on Monday, 2 November 2026
term ends for the Christmas school holidays on Friday, 18 December 2026 with schools closed up to and including Monday, 4 January 2027
Spring term 2027
term starts on Tuesday, 5 January 2027
half-term is Monday, 15 February to Friday, 19 February 2027
term starts again on Monday, 22 February 2027
term ends on Thursday, 25 March 2027
Friday, 26 March and Monday, 29 March 2027 are bank holidays - schools are closed
half-term is Tuesday, 30 March to Friday, 9 April 2027
Monday, 12 April 2027 is a teacher training day - schools are closed
Summer term 2027
term starts on Tuesday, 13 April 2027
early May bank holiday is Monday, 3 May 2027 - schools are closed
term ends on Friday, 28 May 2027
spring bank holiday is Monday, 31 May 2027 - schools are closed
half-term is Tuesday, 1 June to Friday, 4 June 2027
Monday, 7 June 2027 is a teacher training day - schools are closed
term starts again on Tuesday, 8 June 2027
term ends on Wednesday, 21 July 2027
