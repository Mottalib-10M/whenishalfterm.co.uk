Source: https://www.bcpcouncil.gov.uk/schools-and-learning/school-term-and-holiday-dates

School term and holiday dates are usually set over 14 months before the academic year starts. They are based on a school year of 195 days.
Schools can decide to operate different term dates. Schools can also use five days during the school year for staff training.
Please contact your child’s school to find out which dates they are operating.
From 2026 to 2027
Autumn term: 1 September to 18 December
Autumn half term break: 26 October to 30 October
Christmas holiday: 21 December to 3 January
Spring term: 4 January to 25 March
Spring half term break: 15 February to 19 February
Easter holiday: 26 March to 11 April
Summer term: 12 April to 21 July
Summer half term break: 31 May to 4 June
Summer holiday: from 22 July
