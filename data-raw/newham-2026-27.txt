School term and holiday dates Academic Year 2026/2027 
Governing bodies are responsible for setting the term and holiday dates for their schools. This calendar 
provides a framework to support governing bodies in determining those dates. Individual school dates may 
vary, so parents and carers should check the relevant school's website for confirmed dates.

Additional information 
• Maintained schools must be open for 190 teaching days (380 sessions) each academic year in 
accordance with the Education (School Day and School Year) Regulations 1999. 
• This proposed calendar provides for 195 school days, with flexibility for governing boards to designate 
up to two additional school days (up to a maximum of 197 school days). 
• This flexibility may be used to accommodate religious observance or to extend the Christmas or 
summer holiday period. 
• Five days should be identified for staff training (INSET). Pupils do not attend school on these days. 
INSET may be delivered as full days or through an equivalent number of twilight sessions.
