Check the term dates and holiday dates for community schools in the city, maintained by the council.
About these dates
These dates apply to community schools in Brighton & Hove, maintained by the council.
They may not apply to:
academies
free schools
sixth form colleges
further education colleges
schools in the independent sector
You can download the latest school term calendar dates or see a list below.
School year 2026 to 2027
Autumn 2026
Term starts on Wednesday 2 September for most pupils
Tuesday 1 September is usually an INSET day, but check with individual schools
Half term is Monday 26 October to Friday 30 October
Term ends on Friday 18 December
Spring 2027
Term starts on Monday 4 January
Half term is Monday 15 February to Friday 19 February
Term ends on Thursday 25 March
Summer 2027
Term starts on Monday 12 April
Half term is Tuesday 1 June to Friday 4 June
Term ends on Wednesday 21 July
Five days are allowed for in-service training (INSET) for staff. These are published on individual school websites.
