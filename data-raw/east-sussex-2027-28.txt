Accessibility
If you use assistive technology these documents might not be fully accessible. We have included a text alternative for the information. If you need further assistance, contact:
Customer Relations
phone: 0345 60 80 192, option 5. Monday to Friday, 10am to 3pm.
email: Customer Relations Team
The term and holiday dates on this page only apply to:
community schools (run by the council)
voluntary controlled schools (faith schools run by the council)
They may not apply to voluntary aided schools; academies; free schools; sixth form colleges; further education colleges; schools in the independent sector (private schools).
To confirm your school's term dates, check with their website or contact them: Find local schools
INSET training days
Schools are responsible for setting their own INSET days. To confirm your child's INSET days, contact the school or check their website: Find local schools
School closures
Sometimes schools may close at short notice. This can happen during an emergency or in bad weather. You can check if your school is closed on: School closures
Term-time holidays
Information on taking your child out of school during term time is available on Holidays in term time .
You might get a penalty notice if you do not ensure your child goes to school.
Funded holiday clubs
If your school-aged child receives free school meals, you can apply for a free place at a holiday club. A small number of places are available for other eligible groups.
For more information, see: Welcome to Holiday food and fun (HAF)
