Schools that do not follow these term dates
Some schools set their own term dates which may not be the same as those published here:
academies
free schools
foundation schools
faith schools (voluntary-aided schools)
If you are unsure about your school's term dates contact them directly.
Training days
Each school will also be closed for 5 training days a year. Training days are different for each school. Find out when they are happening by contacting your school directly .
