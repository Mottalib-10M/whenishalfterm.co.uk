Term and holiday dates for Warwickshire schools
Setting term and holiday dates
Warwickshire County Council sets term and holiday dates for all community and voluntary controlled schools.
Academies, foundation, and aided schools set their own dates, and we work with them to keep dates as similar as possible.
School dates change each year. We set them based on these parameters:
195 school days - five are teacher training days
Two training days set by the council, three by schools
Holidays in full weeks where possible
A three-weekend break at Christmas and Easter
A summer holiday close to six weeks
A pattern as similar as possible to neighbouring councils
Dates are usually published 18 months before the academic year starts. For example, dates for 2025/26 were published by March 2024.

Induction days
There is one induction day for all Warwickshire schools:
2026 - Wednesday 8 July
2027 - Wednesday 7 July
2028 - Wednesday 5 July
If your child attends an academy, independent school, or a school outside of Warwickshire, their induction date may differ. Check with your child’s current school.
Teacher training days
Most schools have five teacher training days each year, when pupils do not attend school.
We set two:
2025/26 - 1 September 2025 and 20 July 2026
2026/27 - 1 September 2026 and 4 January 2027
2027/28 - 1 September 2027 and 5 January 2028
Schools set three more days each year. Contact your school for these dates.
Academies and free schools set their own training days.
