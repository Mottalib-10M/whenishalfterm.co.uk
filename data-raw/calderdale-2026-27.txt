School term dates
Find school term dates for 2026/27 and 2027/28.
Updated: 2nd September 2026
These term dates apply to community, voluntary controlled and special schools only.
Please note : Academy, foundation and voluntary aided schools set their own term dates and you should contact them for details.
2026 to 2027
2026 to 2027
2026
1st September - Opens for Autumn term.
23rd October - Closes for Autumn half-term.
2nd November - Re-opens after Autumn half-term.
18th December - Closes for the end of Autumn term.
2027
4th January - Opens for Spring term.
12th February - Closes for Spring half-term.
22nd February - Re-opens after Spring half-term.
25th March - Closes for the end of Spring term.
12th April - Opens for Summer term.
3rd May - Closed: May Day.
28th May - Closes for Summer half-term.
7th June - Re-opens after Summer half-term.
21st July - Closes for the end of Summer term
Important note
These are the Council's recommended term dates for schools in the Calderdale area. Please note : Some schools may choose to adopt different dates.
Schools will also be closed to pupils for five School Training/Professional Development Days. Each school will choose when these will be. Please note : You should contact the school to find out when pupils will not attend.
Dates to remember
2027
