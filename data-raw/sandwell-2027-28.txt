School term dates and holidays 2027/28
Here are the school term dates and holidays for Sandwell's primary, secondary and special schools for 2027/28.
Some academies and voluntary aided church schools may not follow this schedule. You are advised to check with these schools directly.
We strongly recommend that you check exact dates with your school - especially before you book a holiday.
Autumn term 2027
Term starts: Thursday 2 September 2027
Half-term holiday: Monday 25 October to Friday 29 October 2027
Term ends: Friday 17 December 2027
Christmas holiday: Monday 20 December 2027 to Monday 3 January 2028
Spring term 2028
Term starts: Tuesday 4 January 2028
Half-term holiday: Monday 14 February to Friday 18 February 2028
Term ends: Friday 7 April 2028
Easter Holiday: Monday 10 April to Friday 21 April 2028
Summer term 2028
Term starts: Monday 24 April 2028 (Remember the Early May Bank Holiday on Monday 1 May 2028)
Half-term holiday: Monday 29 May (Late May Bank Holiday) to Friday 2 June 2028
Term ends: Friday 21 July 2028
Summer holiday starts: Monday 24 July 2028
Schools can issue fines to parents who take their children out of school during term time .
