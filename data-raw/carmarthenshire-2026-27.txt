Designated INSET Days
Tuesday 1 September 2026
Wednesday 1 September 2027
For school specific INSET days, please contact the relevant school. Usually, schools have 5 INSET days during the academic year (including the designated days).
Following a recent consultation, Welsh Government intends to take forward legislation to implement the proposal to maintain a sixth INSET day on an ongoing basis, and issue revised guidance to the sector, subject to ministerial decision after the 2026 election, by the beginning of the 2026/27 academic year.
