Inset and flexible days
These dates provide 197 days. The statutory requirement is for pupils to attend for 190 days.
This allows five days for Inset and two additional days holiday for Eid Al Fitr and Eid Al Adha. These are not specified as they are moveable holidays (see provisional Eid dates and bank holidays list).
