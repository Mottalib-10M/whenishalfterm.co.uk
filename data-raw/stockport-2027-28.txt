2027 to 2028 term dates
Term dates may differ for voluntary aided schools and schools with Trust or Academy status, as these schools are able to set their own calendar.
You'll be able to get further information about school terms dates and start and finish times by contacting the individual school.
Autumn term 2027
Term starts: Wednesday 1 September 2027
Half term holiday: Monday 25 October to Friday 29 October 2027
Term ends: Friday 17 December 2027
Spring term 2028
Term starts: Tuesday 4 January 2028
Half term holiday: Monday 14 February to Friday 18 February 2028
Term ends: Friday 31 March 2028
Easter Public Holidays: Good Friday 14 April and Easter Monday 17 April 2028
Summer term 2028
Term starts: Tuesday 18 April 2028
May Day bank holiday: Monday 1 May 2028
Spring bank holiday: Monday 29 May 2028
Primary half term holiday: Monday 29 May to Friday 9 June 2028
Secondary half term holiday: Monday 29 May to Friday 2 June 2028
Primary term ends: Friday 28 July 2028
Secondary term ends: Friday 21 July 2028
