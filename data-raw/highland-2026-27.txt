The school year runs from August to July the following year.

Holidays
There are holidays during:
October for 2 weeks
Christmas and New Year for 2 weeks
Easter for 2 weeks
Summer for 6 to 8 weeks
In service days
On in-service days, schools are closed to students. These days are often used for training, administrative tasks, or to plan for the upcoming term.
