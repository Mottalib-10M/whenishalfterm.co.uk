How we calculate term dates
The school term dates are based on dates agreed by the London Inter Authority Admissions Group (LIAAG).
The timetable allows for the legal 195 school days. This includes the 190 days when the school must be open to pupils and 5 staff training days. The 5 staff training and development days are decided by the school's governing body according to the school's development planning cycle.
