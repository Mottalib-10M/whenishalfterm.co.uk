Term 2027/28 will commence on Monday 6th September 2027.  The probable Eid dates are Saturday 26th February 2028 & Friday 5th May 2028. The 
Council normally expects schools, with a significant number of pupils who will be celebrating Eid, to consider this when determining their dates.
