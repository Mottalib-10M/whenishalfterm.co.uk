Term dates
Term dates
Find out term dates for Leicester’s schools.
All maintained and voluntary controlled schools in the city follow the dates set by Leicester City Council.
Academies and free schools can set their own term dates.
Schools are allowed to choose five days for staff training, known as inset days.
