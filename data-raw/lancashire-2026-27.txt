School term dates and holidays
These are the school holiday dates we have set as guidance for Lancashire schools.
Individual school holidays may vary from this framework.
Check your child's school calendar before booking a holiday or event. If a school calendar is not available here, contact the school or check their website.
Schools must choose their five training days from the number of openings provided. These are called in-service education and training (INSET) days.
