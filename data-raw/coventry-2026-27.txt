School term dates and holidays
Term dates for Coventry's schools are set up to three years in advance.
Who decides term dates?
School terms and holiday dates are set by the local authority (LA) each year in consultation with parents, headteachers, governing bodies, professional associations, Diocesan Boards of Education, FE Colleges and Trades Unions.

Staff training
During the academic year, a total of 195 days are defined as 'school days.' Pupils must attend school for 190 days and the remaining 5 days are reserved for professional training of school staff. These days are set at each school individually so please contact your school to find out when these dates are.

School holiday activities
Family Information Service directory
Holiday activities are added on a daily basis on our Family Information Directory what's on section [https://cid.coventry.gov.uk/kb5/coventry/directory/whats_on.page] , where you can search by days of the week or across the whole of the holidays.
The Family Information Service maintains a comprehensive directory on behalf of the Local Authority of up-to-date information on local organisations who can offer general help and advice, support services, as well as local leisure activities and facilities.
Childcare and family support
We also provide information on registered childcare and education services for children, young people, parents, carers and professional who support families.
The information is updated on a regular basis for accuracy, but please contact us if you cannot find what you are looking for, or if you would like to be added to the directory.
You can e-mail a request for childcare by completing this online form [/fiscontact] or via the Coventry's Family Information Directory [https://cid.coventry.gov.uk/kb5/coventry/directory/family.page?familychannel=0] website.

Teacher training days
Each individual school is able to allocate a small number of days which are reserved for the professional training of school staff and are set at each school's discretion. Contact your school directly [https://www.coventry.gov.uk/directory/10/schools_and_their_contact_details] to find out when these will be.
