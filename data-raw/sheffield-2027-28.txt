All about Sheffield's school calendar.
We're responsible for setting term dates for community and voluntary controlled schools, including special schools.
All schools must provide 190 days of education a year.
A school list with contact details for all schools in Sheffield is also available on this page.

Schools also need to have 5 teacher professional development days. Professional development days are not fixed, and each school will decide for itself when and how to take these days. Schools may close for a full day, part of a day or have all of their professional development training after normal school hours. Your school will tell you if they will affect your child.
We use a common set of term dates across the city, but schools that have their own Admission Authority can set their own term dates and our calendars may not apply to them.

School closures for religious observance
If your school plans to close for religious holidays then they will let you know ahead of time and will ensure that all 190 teaching days are achieved.

CONSULTATION ON PROPOSED SCHOOL CALENDAR 2027/28 AND YEAR 7 
TRANSITION DAYS  
 
FOR INFORMATION & ACTION 
 
Section 32 of the Education Act 2002 requires the Local Authority to determine the dates when the school 
terms and holidays are to begin and end for schools under its control (i.e. Community and Voluntary 
Controlled Schools) for the 2027/28 academic year. 
 
Schools that are their own Admission Authorities may set their own term dates.  It is however desirable if 
there is a common set of dates across the City, at least for the main holidays. 
 
Headteachers and Governors are invited to respond to the proposed term dates for the 2027/28 academic 
year.  The proposed calendar will also be placed on the Council website, but it would be helpful if you 
could also circulate the proposal to your parents and carers or place on the school website. 
 
The school calendar must consist of 195 days, 5 of which are designated teacher training days.  The Local 
Authority’s proposed term dates for 2027/28 are attached at Appendix A. 
 
There continues to be a high level of support for the fixing of the Easter Break at the beginning of 
April the Authority is consulting on a single model which fixes the Easter break.  
 
The Easter Bank holidays in 2028 are Friday 14 April and Monday 17 April.  The last day of term will be 
Friday 31 March 2028. 
 
A final decision on the term dates will be taken by Education, Children and Families Committee, your 
responses are important so please take the opportunity to contribute to the final calendar.  The deadline 
for responses is 20 February 2026. 
 
The Authority also proposes transition dates for pupils prior to secondary transfer.  It has become more 
common for schools to make their own arrangements for transition days.  Historically the first Wednesday 
Thursday of July was designated for this purpose to promote greater consistency across the City.  Further 
details are provided below. 
 
 
 
 

Current Principles Behind Setting Term Dates 
 
The key principles to setting the calendar are: 
 
•       The Autumn half term break will be the five days which are the last period Monday to Friday 
in October/November. According to the year, October half term can start as early as Monday 21 
and as late as Monday 27 October.  
 
• The Christmas break will be at least 10 weekdays, and will normally start at the end of the school 
day on the Friday, which is on, or otherwise immediately prior to, 22 December. It will include the 
New Year’s day holiday which is outside that period, and the day after New Years Day, where 
this is a weekday. 
 
• The February half term holiday will be five days Monday to Friday, which divides the term as far 
as possible into two equal parts. Where there are an odd number of weeks the second half term 
will be the shorter of the two. 
 
• The Easter break will normally be fixed at the first two weeks in late March or beginning of April 
to support the creation of more even half terms. 
 
• The Spring Bank half term will be five days Monday to Friday from the statutory holiday which 
falls as the last Monday in May. 
 
• Where a term is of uneven length, the second half term will be the shorter of the two.

Transition Days 
 
The Authority proposes to recommend specific dates for transition visits prior to the transfer to 
secondary schools.  This proposal has been historically advocated by Headteacher representative 
groups.  For the 2027/28 September intake the proposed transition days are Wednesday 5th and 
Thursday 6th July 2028.   
 
This should give sufficient time for all schools to set these days in their planning arrangements for 
2027/28.  Please note that these dates are not compulsory, they are recommended in order to 
streamline the transition process, but schools are free to make their own arrangements. 
 
Religious Observance 
 
All schools must provide 190 teaching days and 5 development days.  Within the school calendar 
however there may be flexibility to enable schools to observe religious observance both for pupils and 
staff.   
 
The Executive Director and Lead Cabinet Member are keen to ensure that there is sufficient flexibility 
within the school calendar to accommodate religious observance and are keen to receive your 
comments on how such arrangements may be accommodated.  I attach a briefing note (Appendix B) 
in which there are some suggestions to facilitate religious observance based on practices in other 
Authorities and current practice in some of our own schools. 
 
Any days proposed for closure due to religious observance should be discussed first with the 
Governing Body taking into account the cohesion of the local community. 
 
What Happens Next? 
 
Following consultation, the final recommended calendar will be presented to the Education & Families 
Committee on 24 March 2026.  Following determination, the approved calendar will be circulated to 
all schools. It will then be a matter for each individual Governing Body of schools and academies that 
are their own admission authorities to determine whether it will adopt the recommended model or set 
its own calendar.  If your Governing Body is considering a significant departure from the recommended 
model, you are advised that you should undertake a full consultation with parents, the Local Authority 
and other interested stakeholders prior to it being determined.   You are advised to complete any 
consultation and set the term dates before the end of the summer term 2026. 
 
If you have any comments on the proposed school calendar or transition dates, I should be pleased 
to receive them by 20 February 2026.
