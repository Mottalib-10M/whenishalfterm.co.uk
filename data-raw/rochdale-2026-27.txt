2026–2027 school holidays
This is our recommended school holiday pattern for September 2026–August 2027.
Autumn term 2026–2027
There are 15 weeks in this school term.
Autumn term begins: Thursday, 3 September 2026
Autumn half-term holiday: Monday, 26 October 2026–Friday, 30 October 2026 inclusive
Autumn term ends: Friday, 18 December 2026
Christmas holiday: Monday, 21 December 2026–Friday, 1 January 2027 inclusive
Spring term 2026–2027
There are 11 weeks in this school term.
Spring term begins: Monday, 4 January 2027
Spring half-term holiday: Monday, 15 February 2027–Friday, 19 February 2027 inclusive
Spring term ends: Thursday, 25 March 2027 (Friday, 26 March 2027 is a public holiday)
Easter holiday: Monday, 29 March 2027–Friday, 9 April 2027 inclusive
Summer term 2026–2027
There are 14 weeks in this school term.
Summer term begins: Monday, 12 April 2027
Summer half-term holiday: Monday, 31 May 2027–Friday, 4 June 2027 inclusive (Monday, 31 May 2027 is a public holiday)
Summer term ends: Wednesday, 21 July 2027
Summer holiday: Thursday, 22 July 2027–Tuesday, 31 August 2027 inclusive

About school terms, holiday dates and closures
We give schools in the borough a recommended school holiday pattern for the academic year.
Check exact dates with your school
Every year schools are required to choose 3 days during term time to set aside for staff training which students don't attend.
We also allow schools to change up to 2 days of our recommended holiday pattern, for example for polling days or religious festivals. You'll need to refer to your specific school's website for the exact term dates.
