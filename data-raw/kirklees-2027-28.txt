These term dates apply to community, voluntary controlled and
special schools.
Some schools may have different dates. Academies, free schools,
foundation and voluntary aided schools can set their own term
dates and they may not be the same as these published dates. If
you are unsure you should check the school's website for further
details or contact the school directly.
School training days
Each school is also closed for 5 training days each year.
Training days are different for each school and you should check
the school's website for further details or contact the school
directly to find out when these are.
