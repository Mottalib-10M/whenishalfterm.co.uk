These dates are for Community and Voluntary Controlled schools. Other types of schools – such as Academies, Voluntary Aided, Foundation and Free Schools can set their own term dates and holidays, but most use the same dates as us.
Each year, schools have 195 days in the calendar. Pupils go to school for 190 of those days. The remaining 5 days are INSET days, which schools can choose to suit their needs. Each school is responsible for notifying parents and staff which dates they have chosen for INSET days.
important
Parents should check the agreed term dates with their children’s school.
