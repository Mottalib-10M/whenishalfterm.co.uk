Schools can take up to five development day closures during an academic year. This may change the start or end date of any school term.
It is important to note that some schools have the freedom to set their own term and holiday dates. Parents and carers must check with their child's school to confirm if they are expected to attend on this particular day.
Academies, private and independent schools have the freedom to determine and set their own term dates/holidays and inset days.
We will continue to set term and holiday dates for community and voluntary controlled schools.
School development day closures
2025 to 2026
It is the responsibility of each
individual school to notify parents and guardians of their development day
closures and to those supplying meals and transport.
Academies are not required to
notify us of development days, though we will publish the information
where known.
If you have any queries regarding the school closure dates shown,
contact your child's school and college directly for clarification in the first
instance.
