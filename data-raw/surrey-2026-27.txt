School term dates
Term dates, including half term, Easter, Christmas and Summer breaks, can vary.
Schools set their own INSET days.
Academies, Voluntary Aided and Foundation schools may have different term dates.
Check your school's details in our Directory of Surrey schools .
See UK bank holidays (GOV.UK)

Academic year 2026 to 2027
From September 2026, community and voluntary controlled schools will have a two-week autumn half term .
Five days will be taken from the summer holiday in July 2027.
The school year will still have 195 days, including 5 INSET days.
Other schools set their own dates but are encouraged to follow these.

Academic year 2028 to 2029
Future dates for the 2028 to 2029 academic year will be published before 10 May 2027.
