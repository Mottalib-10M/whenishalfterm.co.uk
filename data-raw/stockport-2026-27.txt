2026 to 2027 term dates
Term dates may differ for voluntary aided schools and schools with Trust or Academy status, as these schools are able to set their own calendar.
You'll be able to get further information about school terms dates and start and finish times by contacting the individual school.
Autumn term 2026
Term starts: Tuesday 1 September 2026
Half term holiday: Monday 26 October to Friday 30 October 2026
Term ends: Friday 18 December 2026
Spring term 2027
Term starts: Monday 4 January 2027
Half term holiday: Monday 15 February to Friday 19 February 2027
Term ends: Thursday 25 March 2027
Easter public holidays: Good Friday 26 March and Easter Monday 29 March 2027
Summer term 2027
Term starts: Monday 12 April 2027
May Day bank holiday: Monday 3 May 2027
Spring bank holiday: Monday 31 May 2027
Primary half term holiday: Monday 31 May to Friday 11 June 2027
Secondary half term holiday: Monday 31 May to Friday 4 June 2027
Primary term ends: Wednesday 28 July 2027
Secondary term ends: Wednesday 21 July 2027
