School term dates and holidays
Schools are able to consider variations to term dates provided they meet the statutory requirements. Parents are advised to contact the relevant school for confirmation of term dates.

If you're looking for information about inset days or other arrangements you should check with the school directly. You can search for school contact details .
Calendar style term date documents are attached to this page.
Holidays during term time
You should not normally take your child on holiday during term time. GOV.UK has information on what the law says about school attendance and absence .
