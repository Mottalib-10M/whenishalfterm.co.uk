This page gives a brief overview of the terms dates within Doncaster. Individual dates may vary: For Academies and Church Schools - please check with the School. You will find information about the current and future school holiday dates, and there are also downloadable versions of the school holiday calendars available in the downloads section of this page.
The Council is responsible for determining school holiday patterns for its maintained schools.
Following consultation on previous school holiday patterns the Council agreed to set the late spring holiday, normally around Easter, to cover the majority of the first two weeks of April with the remainder following the traditional holiday pattern.
City of Doncaster Council is seeking colleague’s views on the 2027/28 School Holiday Pattern. Two versions are being considered and are attached for your information.
Closing Date – 24 th April 2026

City of Doncaster Council (CDC) is responsible for determining the holiday pattern for community primary schools, community special schools and Pupil Referral Units within the City and these are set out at the end of this document.
Academies, Free Schools and Church Schools will set their own dates and are encouraged to set the same or similar dates.
Drafting and Consultation
Initial discussions regarding proposals were held with colleagues from the 3 other South Yorkshire Councils who have now set a common holiday pattern (Version 1 of CDC proposals). Following input from the wider Yorkshire and Humber Region, a second version has also been drafted which sets the commencement date on the first Monday in September and consequently has a later finish in the summer term.
The two draft patterns were considered by the Children, Young People and Schools Directorate Leadership Team who agreed to consult the following groups on the two options before making a final decision:
