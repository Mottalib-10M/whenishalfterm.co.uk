School holiday and term dates
This page shows term and holiday dates for Nottinghamshire schools that are maintained by us.
Foundation, trust, voluntary aided schools and academies set their own term dates. Check holiday dates and INSET days on your school's own website .
For Nottingham City schools, visit Nottingham City Council's website
