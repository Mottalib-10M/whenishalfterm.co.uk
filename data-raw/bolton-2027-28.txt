Dates for 2027/28 holiday pattern 

Term dates may vary slightly for each school, please check with individual schools for their holiday dates. (Term dates are published on school websites)

Autumn Term 	Total 70 days in term 

Term begins: 	Monday 6th September 2027
Half Term: 		Monday 25th October – Friday 29th October 2027
Term ends: 		Friday 17th December 2027

Spring Term 	Total 59 days in term 

Term begins: 	Tuesday 4th January 2028
Half Term: 		Monday 21st February – Friday 25th February 2028
Term ends: 		Friday 31st March 2028

Summer Term 	Total 63 days in term 

Term begins: 	Tuesday 18th April 2028
Bank Holiday: 	Monday 1st May 2028
Half Term: 		Monday 29th May (Bank holiday) – Friday 2nd June 2028

Term ends: 		Friday 21st July 2028

Additional Information: 

	•	Children will be in school for 190 days. 
	•	Staff will be in school for 195 days.
	•	5 INSET days to be allocated by the school during non-term time. 
	•	The above pattern equates to 192 days and Governing Boards have the authority to determine 2 occasional days to meet the local needs of the school.
	•	There is a 6-week summer break leading into this year with a 6-week break at the end.
	•	Start of next academic year is Monday 4th September 2028.
