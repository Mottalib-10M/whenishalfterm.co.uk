School term dates for Community and Voluntary Controlled schools.
Schools may choose to hold a teachers' in-service training day (INSET) within term time (up to 5 days per year), so the date on which term ends, or starts, for pupils at different schools may change.
You should contact your child's school for their precise term dates.
