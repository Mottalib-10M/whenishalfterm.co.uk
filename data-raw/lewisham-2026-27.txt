School term dates for Lewisham schools, how many days a year children go to school, and what happens when schools are used as polling stations.
School term dates 2026-27
Autumn term
Wednesday 2 September to Friday 23 October (38 days)
Half term: Monday 26 October to Friday 30 October
Monday 2 November to Friday 18 December (35 days)
Spring term
Monday 4 January to Friday 12 February (30 days)
Half term: Monday 15 February to Friday 19 February
Monday 22 February to Thursday 25 March (24 days)
Summer term
Monday 12 April to Friday 28 May (34 days) (the early May bank holiday will be taken on 3 May)
Half term: Monday 31 May to Friday 4 June
Monday 7 June to Thursday 22 July (34 days)
Total: 195
In 2026–27 children should attend school for 190 days (or 380 half sessions). Teachers must be available to work for 195 days during the academic year.
Schools which close for local elections must extend a term or coincide the closure with an INSET day.
