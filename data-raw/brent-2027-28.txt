2027/28
Autumn Term 2027 (73 days)
- First half: Wednesday 1 September to Friday 22 October 2027
- Half term: Monday 25 October to Friday 29 October 2027
- Second half: Monday 1 November to Friday 17 December 2027
- Christmas holiday: Monday 20 December 2027 to Monday 3 January 2028
- Bank holidays: Monday 27 December 2027, Tuesday 28 December 2027, Monday 3 January 2028
Spring Term 2028 (59 days)
- First half: Tuesday 4 January to Friday 11 February 2028
- Half term: Monday 14 February to Friday 18 February 2028
- Second half: Monday 21 February to Friday 31 March 2028
- School holidays: Monday 3 April to Monday 17 April 2028
- Bank holidays: Friday 14 April 2028, Monday 17 April 2028
Summer Term 2028 (63+3 days)
- First half: Tuesday 18 April to Friday 26 May 2028
- Half term: Monday 29 May to Friday 2 June 2028
- Second half: Monday 5 June to Wednesday 26 July 2028
- Bank holidays: Monday 1 May 2028, Monday 29 May 2028
Notes: The year totals 198 days, with the same split as 2026/27: schools must open for 195, including 190 pupil days, 5 INSET days, and 3 optional religious-festival closure days. Schools that don't take the optional closures should finish on Friday 21 July 2028.
