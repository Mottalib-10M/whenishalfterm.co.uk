School term and holiday dates
We publish school term and holiday dates for community and voluntary controlled schools in North Yorkshire.
Different term dates
Some schools have slightly different term dates to those below, whilst others including academy, voluntary aided and foundation schools set all of their own dates. Schools also set a further two professional development training dates of their own.
You should contact your school to confirm your dates .
